The Rare Earth of Desire.
A substance, needed for subsistence—dense, and danced.
The breed of red flowers grows from its adhesion to all that once was beauty.
It doesn’t destroy; it corrodes slowly, like rancor.
It dwells in the wires, the networks, the archives.
It has memory, but no time.

I
There is no time on the land of oblivion. The time has been devoured on its annihilation. The Humani race spoke about The Ruins. But there is only one truth about the cosmogony of Arka. The time in history is lived on the peoples hearts of Arka. Celestials discover the souls of the primigenious people of Arka, the Druids.

II 
The druids use the Armilar Camp to study the secrets of the Cosmos. In the stars they discovered the existence of Celestials thousands of years before the Arrival, when the Cloud City of Celestica descended from the sky.
